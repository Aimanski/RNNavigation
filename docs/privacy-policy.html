WeighApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

WeighApp ("we", "us", "our") is a meal planning and weight tracking application that helps users log daily meals, track calories and macros, monitor weight progress, log sleep, and record blood pressure readings. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and a username. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's email address and basic profile information (name) via Google's OAuth sign-in — we do not receive your Google password.

Health and fitness information you provide. To calculate personalized recommendations, we collect information you choose to enter, including age, height, weight, gender, and activity level. From these we calculate BMI, BMR, and daily calorie/macro targets.

Usage and activity data. We store the meals and foods you log (including calories, protein, carbs, and fat), your daily calorie progress, sleep logs (sleep time, wake time, and duration), and blood pressure readings you choose to record.

App preferences. We store settings such as reminder preferences and whether you've completed the app's onboarding tutorial.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core app functionality — tracking your meals, weight, sleep, and calorie goals
  • To calculate personalized calorie and macro recommendations
  • To maintain your day-streak and progress history
  • To send reminders you've configured within the app
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

WeighApp's data is stored using Supabase, a backend infrastructure provider, on servers operated by Supabase. Data is transmitted between the app and our servers using encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In — to let you create an account and log in using your Google account. We only request access to your basic email and profile information.
  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider, which stores your account and app data on our behalf.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not share it with advertisers.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below. Once we process a deletion request, your data is permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Health Information Disclaimer

WeighApp is a self-tracking tool intended for general wellness purposes. It is not a medical device, and the calorie, macro, and health-related calculations it provides are estimates, not medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making decisions about your diet, exercise, or health, especially if you have a pre-existing medical condition.

7. Children's Privacy

WeighApp is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Choices

You can review and update the personal information in your profile directly within the app at any time. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we make changes. Continued use of WeighApp after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
